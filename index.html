What this is

YouTube Stats is a private tool that EV1LGENERAL uses to read the statistics of his own YouTube channels, such as views, watch time and subscriber numbers, so he can see how his videos are doing.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no accounts for other people and nothing for visitors to use. It only ever reads data from channels that the signed-in owner manages, with read-only access, and the results stay on the owner's own computer.
