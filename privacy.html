About YouTube Stats

YouTube Stats is a private tool run by EV1LGENERAL on his own computer. It reads the statistics of the YouTube channels he owns. It is not offered to the public, and the only person who signs in to it is the channel owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the tool uses read-only access to the YouTube Data API, the YouTube Analytics API and the YouTube Reporting API. It reads statistics (for example views, watch time, subscribers, performance reports and video details) of the channels that the signed-in owner manages. It cannot change, upload, delete or post anything on a channel.

Where the data goes

  • The statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • They are never sold, shared, rented or sent to any third party.
  • They are not used for advertising, and no person other than the owner reads them.
  • This website itself has no analytics, cookies, trackers or forms, and collects nothing about its visitors.

Google API Services User Data Policy

YouTube Stats's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The tool also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by selecting YouTube Stats and choosing to remove access. The owner can also delete the locally stored statistics from his computer at any time.
